# 9. What Makes Steal a Soul Different

Steal an Egg and Steal a Brainrot are **races**: grab, sprint, done. Steal a Soul keeps the steal-and-hatch loop players love but turns every trip into a **heist**: you plan, sneak, use powers, and outsmart guardians and other players.

| | Steal an Egg / Brainrot | **Steal a Soul** |
|---|---|---|
| Getting the loot | Run in, grab, run out | **Sneak past vision cones**, hide behind cover, or go loud and use powers |
| Guardians | Chase the closest player | **See and hear you**: line of sight, vision cone, glow of rare souls, "!" spotted / "?" searching, call for backup |
| Your pets | Sit and earn | Earn **and** one follows you as a **Companion with a power** |
| Duplicates | Sell them | **Soul Fusion**: 3 spirits become 1 of the next rarity (can keep mutations) |
| Getting robbed | Chase the thief | **Haunt** the thief (curse that slows them) and bonk them |
| Night | Mostly cosmetic | Darker = easier to sneak, but faster guardians and better souls |

## 9.1 Stealth Heists 🕯️
- Guardians see in a **~150° cone** in front of them and need **line of sight**. Rocks, ruins and walls block it.
- Anyone within **10 studs** is heard from any direction (only **4** while sneaking).
- **Sneak (C or button):** move at 60% speed, and guardians only see you at 55% of their normal range.
- **Rare souls glow:** every rarity tier you carry makes you visible from 6% further away. A Secret soul is a lighthouse.
- **Night:** sight range −15% (darker) while guardians move faster, so it's a gamble.
- **Spotted ("!"):** the guardian chases and **alerts others within 45 studs**. Break line of sight for **2.5s** and it loses you ("?"), checks your last position, then walks home.

This makes skill matter: a good sneaky player with low Speed can rob a biome that a fast-but-loud player can't.

## 9.2 Companion Spirits & Powers ✨
Press **Follow** on any spirit in the Spirits menu. It floats beside you (everyone can see it) and gives you its biome's power on **Q**:

| Companion from | Power | Effect | Base cooldown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whispering Meadow | Wisp Rush | +35% speed for 3s | 18s |
| Ember Hollow | Flame Dash | Burst forward | 14s |
| Frostveil Peaks | Frost Nova | Freeze guardians within 30 studs for 3s | 25s |
| Sunken Crypt | Grave Veil | Guardians can't see you for 4s | 28s |
| Storm Spire | Lightning Step | Huge dash + 20% speed | 16s |
| Void Rift | Shadow Cloak | Invisible to guardians **and players** for 5s | 32s |
| Celestial Garden | Halo Ward | Blocks the next catch or bonk (8s) | 30s |
| Abyssal Throne | Reaper's Rend | Freeze nearby guardians + 40% speed | 35s |

Rarer companions recharge faster (−6% per rarity tier; mutated −10% more). This gives **low-income spirits a reason to exist** (a Common Void spirit still has Shadow Cloak) and makes collecting across biomes strategic.

## 9.3 Soul Fusion 🔮
In the Spirits menu: **3 spirits of the same rarity → 1 soul of the next rarity**, from the best biome among the three. It has a **50% chance to keep the best mutation**. The fused soul has to incubate (so it can be stolen!) before it hatches. Uses your 3 lowest-earning, non-vaulted, non-companion inventory spirits.

## 9.4 Haunt Revenge 👻
When someone steals from your base you get **20 seconds** to press **HAUNT THIEF (H)**: the thief is slowed to 55% for 5s and glows purple. That's your window to catch them and bonk the soul loose. Getting robbed now feels like the start of a chase, not just a loss.

- **Thumbnail idea:** a player crouched behind a rock, a guardian's red "!" just above it, a glowing Mythic soul in hand and a companion spirit beside them.
- **Trailer beats (30s):** sneak past cone → spotted "!" → Frost Nova freeze → escape → hatch → friend steals it → HAUNT → bonk → steal it back.
